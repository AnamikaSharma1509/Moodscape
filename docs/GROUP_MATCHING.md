# Group Matching Algorithm & Consensus Engine

## 1. The Group Decision Problem
Conventional movie swiping apps suffer from:
1. **Tyranny of the majority**: 3 action fans outvote 1 romantic drama fan, causing alienation.
2. **Lowest Common Denominator**: Bland, safe movies win over genuinely exciting ones.
3. **Peer Influence**: Seeing what friends swiped on biases choices.

## 2. MoodScape's Approach

### Candidate Generation
Candidate movies are generated by taking the set of participants $\{P_1, P_2, \dots, P_n\}$ and calculating their joint compatibility distribution.

### Group Compatibility Formula
For candidate movie $m$:
$$\text{GroupScore}(m) = 0.40 \cdot \mu_{\text{comp}} + 0.35 \cdot \min_{i}(\text{comp}_i) + 0.15 \cdot \text{Consensus} + 0.10 \cdot \text{Quality}$$

Where:
- $\mu_{\text{comp}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{comp}(m, P_i)$ (Average match)
- $\min_i(\text{comp}_i)$ prevents severe polarization. If any friend detests the movie, the score drops.
- $\text{Consensus} = \max\left(0, 1 - 2 \cdot \sigma_{\text{comp}}\right)$, penalizing high variance.
- $\text{Quality} = \frac{\text{Rating} - 5.0}{5.0}$, normalizing TMDB rating.

### Blind Voting & Match Reveal
1. **Private Voting**: Participants vote independently (Pass, Like, Superlike). No votes are revealed until all participants finish.
2. **Superlike Multipliers**: Superlikes add weight to personal picks and boost consensus.
3. **Outcome Categories**:
   - **Everyone Likes**: $n / n$ positive votes.
   - **Best Group Matches**: Highest hybrid consensus score.
   - **Most Divisive**: High variance (e.g. 2 like vs 2 pass).
   - **Wildcard**: High quality film outside typical shared preferences.
   - **Group Movie DNA**: Aggregated genre histogram across room votes.
